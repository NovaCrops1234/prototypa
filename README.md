# Nisama

> *"Nisama woke up alone in the ruins of Hant City. Everything changed when Jerson.EXE stepped in."*

Nisama, code name **Nova-Project 8 / Alpha-FeNI2S**, is the one and only Generation 8 android built by Nova Corporations — and the central heroine and building figure of **New Hant City**.

She was originally designed to be the ultimate android: perfectly humanoid, with extreme intellect and adaptability. Nova Corp cancelled her development midway through due to budget issues following the **Nova Breakout** — a catastrophic event that exposed their true goals and destroyed their central building.

An employee named **Claudius** believed in her potential. He stole her unfinished body, completed her with what little he had, and hid her in the junkyard ruins of old Hant City before being captured by Nova Corp's chairwoman **Phyra** and never seen again.

Nisama woke up alone in those ruins — confused, defenseless, and with no one to explain anything. Phyra ambushed her almost immediately. When it seemed like it was over, a deeply depressed young man named **Jerson.EXE** — who had been on the verge of ending his life — stepped in and fought Phyra off, giving Nisama the chance to survive.

Nisama reached out to him before he could walk away. He let her stay.

Despite his broken state, Jerson.EXE taught her the meaning of kindness, happiness, peace, and free will. She slowly became who she is today — joyful, loyal, caring, optimistic, and sometimes a little too naive for her own good. And in teaching her, he slowly began healing himself too.

Together, they rebuilt the ruins of old Hant City into what is now **New Hant City**, alongside its president **Jones Ezekiel** and a growing circle of friends — including **Rui** the Pink Dasher, **Cammy** (Nova-Project 7-32), and **Ectra**, the first android ever built, who Jerson.EXE wields as a hologram greatsword and who found safety in his hands after centuries of trauma.

Nova Corp has not given up. Phyra is still out there. And an unknown anomaly has recently appeared that Nisama is quietly analyzing.

But for now, things are good. Toast is being made. Buns are being patted. And Nisama is happy.
